## About the Project

CivicFix is a community-focused digital platform designed to bridge the gap between residents and municipalities by making infrastructure problem reporting and service delivery more transparent and efficient.

Growing up, I witnessed how neglected infrastructure affects people's daily lives. From streets flooded with sewage and streetlights that remained broken for months to dangerous electrical infrastructure, these experiences motivated me to explore how technology can help communities report problems and hold service delivery processes accountable.

CivicFix aims to provide a central platform where residents can report infrastructure problems, submit supporting evidence, identify affected locations and track the progress of their reports until resolution.

## Problem Statement

Many communities experience infrastructure and service delivery challenges, including sewage leaks, potholes, faulty streetlights, electricity faults and inadequate waste management.

Some of these problems go unreported because residents do not know where or how to report them. Others are reported but lack transparent follow-up, leaving residents uncertain about whether their concerns have been acknowledged or addressed.

CivicFix aims to address this gap by creating a more accessible and transparent communication channel between communities and municipal service teams.

## Objectives

* Simplify the process of reporting community infrastructure problems.
* Allow residents to attach photographs and location details to reports.
* Provide unique reference numbers for tracking reported issues.
* Improve visibility into the progress and status of reports.
* Help administrators review, prioritise, assign and manage reported problems.
* Support accountability through resolution records and performance analytics.
* Identify potentially duplicate reports to improve issue management.

## Key Features

The planned features include:

* Infrastructure issue reporting.
* Location-based issue identification and mapping.
* Photo attachments and supporting evidence.
* Unique report reference numbers.
* Report status tracking.
* Administrative dashboard for managing reports.
* Issue categorisation and prioritisation.
* Duplicate report flagging.
* Resolution updates and completion evidence.
* Community service delivery analytics.

## Target Users

**Residents:** People who need a convenient way to report infrastructure problems and follow up on their submissions.

**Municipal administrators and service teams:** Personnel who need to review, assign, prioritise and track reported issues.

**Community organisations:** Organisations that support local development and need better visibility into recurring community infrastructure problems.

## How It Works

The intended reporting workflow is:

1. A resident creates a report describing an infrastructure problem.
2. The resident selects a category and provides the affected location.
3. Supporting photographs can be attached where appropriate.
4. CivicFix generates a unique reference number for tracking.
5. An administrator reviews the report and assigns it to the appropriate service team.
6. The report status is updated as work progresses.
7. The issue is marked as resolved when completion is recorded, with supporting evidence where available.

## Project Scope

The initial version focuses on reporting, tracking and administrative management of community infrastructure problems.

Future enhancements may include:

* AI-assisted report categorisation and summarisation.
* Potential duplicate detection.
* Advanced infrastructure issue analytics.
* Notifications when report statuses change.
* Offline report submission with synchronisation when connectivity returns.
* Integration with municipal systems, subject to access and technical feasibility.
